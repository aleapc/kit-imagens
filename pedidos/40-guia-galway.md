> Fotografia realista de viagem, formato horizontal 3:2 (1200×800), com a luz atlântica mutável do Oeste irlandês — céu de nuvens rápidas, garoa e arco-íris possíveis, tons de pedra cinza, verde intenso das turfeiras, mar azul-acinzentado. Profundidade de campo suave, um ponto focal claro; pessoas de costas, de perfil ou a média distância, nenhum rosto reconhecível em primeiro plano. TEXTO: NÃO invente letras, placas, letreiros, cardápios nem preços legíveis — deixe qualquer superfície escrita ILEGÍVEL (fora de foco, em ângulo, cortada), sem pseudo-escrita; onde a cena pedir superfície escrita, deixe-a desfocada, de viés ou fora do quadro. NENHUMA bandeira estampada, NENHUM brasão legível, NENHUM logotipo de marca, NENHUM personagem de marca registrada. Ambiente de Galway e do Oeste da Irlanda: as fachadas coloridas e as ruas de pedra do Latin Quarter, o Spanish Arch de pedra cinza à beira do rio Corrib, os pubs de música tradicional com portas pintadas, o calçadão de Salthill diante da baía, as falésias de Moher despencando sobre o Atlântico revolto, os muros de pedra seca e os fortes pré-históricos das Ilhas Aran, a Abadia de Kylemore refletida no lago diante das montanhas de Connemara, o planalto lunar de calcário do Burren, castelos à beira-mar, céu mutável e névoa.

A faithful replica of a traditional whitewashed thatched Irish cottage in Cong, its rounded thatch, red half-door and small windows set among green trees and flowers. A recreated rustic interior suggested through the doorway; soft Irish light, a picturesque nostalgic film-heritage cottage.

### gal_renvyle.webp
**Península de Renvyle**

The quiet Renvyle peninsula north of Letterfrack, a curving sandy beach and coastal lake with the ruins of an O'Flaherty castle by the shore, the island of Inishbofin low on the horizon. Marram grass and dark rock in the foreground; a wide Atlantic sky at sunset, warm light on the sea, remote and tranquil.

### gal_ross_errilly.webp
**Ross Errilly Friary**

The remarkably complete medieval Ross Errilly Friary standing isolated in flat farmland near Headford, its intact church, cloister and tower of weathered grey stone rising from green fields. Empty meadows all around, no services in sight; a big moody sky, the solitude and integrity of the ruin part of its power.

### gal_roundstone.webp

The fishing village of Roundstone with its pretty harbour of moored boats facing the mountains, rows of low colourful houses along the waterfront. The quartzite Twelve Bens rising behind the bay; soft westerly light on the calm water, a picturesque and peaceful Connemara coastal scene.

### gal_salmon_weir_bridge.webp

The historic stone Salmon Weir Bridge over the fast-flowing river Corrib beside Galway Cathedral, now a pedestrian and cycle crossing. ### gal_salthill_hotel.webp

A seafront hotel facing Galway Bay directly across the Salthill promenade, many of its windows looking out over the water and the distant Aran Islands. The Prom and the bay in front; warm light from the hotel bar at golden hour, a comfortable base by the Atlantic under a colourful evening sky.

### gal_salthill_prom.webp

The 2-kilometre Salthill promenade curving along the edge of Galway Bay, walkers and joggers strolling with the wide sea to one side and the low hills of the Burren far across the water. A brisk Atlantic breeze, scattered cloud and shifting light; benches facing the bay, the everyday seafront heart of Galway.

### gal_seven_churches.webp

The early-medieval monastic ruins of Na Seacht dTeampaill in the west of Inis Mór, two roofless stone churches with carved crosses and old gravestones among drystone walls. The Atlantic beyond the low fields; weathered grey stone under a breezy island sky, a quiet ancient pilgrimage site.

### gal_shop_quay_street.webp

### gal_silverstrand.webp

The pale-sand sheltered cove of Silverstrand west of Salthill, a Blue Flag family beach with calm water and low dunes, looking out across Galway Bay. Families on the sand in the foreground; the bay and distant hills beyond, a bright fresh sky of sun and cloud, clean light on the light-coloured sand.

### gal_sky_road.webp

The panoramic Sky Road loop climbing above Clifden, a narrow coastal road along a headland with sweeping views over the bay, scattered islands and the open Atlantic. Drystone walls and heather along the roadside; golden evening light on the sea, the ruins of Clifden Castle small below, a wide luminous sky.

### gal_spanish_arch.webp
**Spanish Arch e Long Walk**

The grey stone Spanish Arch, the surviving 16th-century town wall where the river Corrib meets Galway Bay, beside the colourful row of Long Walk houses reflected in the river. ### gal_st_nicholas_church.webp
**Collegiate Church of St Nicholas**

The medieval Collegiate Church of St Nicholas, the largest continuously used medieval church in Ireland, its grey stone tower and Gothic details rising over the old town. Carved gargoyles and weathered stonework; a market stall or two suggested around its base, soft light on the ancient façade under broken cloud.

### gal_synges_chair_inismeain.webp
**Cadeira de Synge (Inis Meáin)**

Synge's Chair, a simple drystone shelter on the wild western edge of Inis Meáin where the playwright sat to watch the sea, looking out over dramatic cliffs and the Atlantic. Bare limestone and drystone walls all around; a windswept island sky, the quietest and most traditional of the Aran Islands, utterly remote.

### gal_teampall_bheanain.webp
**Teampall Bheanáin (Inis Mór)**

Teampall Bheanáin, one of Ireland's smallest stone churches, a tiny early-Christian oratory perched on a rocky hilltop above Kilronan on Inis Mór, unusually aligned north–south. Bare rock and drystone walls around it; a sweeping view over Galway Bay below, low golden evening light, a miniature ancient landmark.

### gal_the_kings_head.webp

### gal_thoor_ballylee.webp

Thoor Ballylee, a 16th-century Norman tower house that the poet W. B. Yeats restored as his summer home, a tall square grey stone tower beside a small stone bridge and stream near Gort. Green fields and trees around it; a soft moody sky, ivy on the walls, a literary landmark reflected in the water.

### gal_ti_joe_wattys.webp

### gal_tigh_neachtain.webp

The unmistakable blue-painted corner façade of Tigh Neachtain pub on Quay Street, a beloved 19th-century Galway trad-music pub with old timber window frames. People with pints at outdoor tables on the corner; the colourful Latin Quarter around it, warm evening light on the distinctive blue paint.

### gal_turoe_stone.webp
**Turoe Stone**

The Turoe Stone, a rounded Iron-Age granite monolith covered in flowing curvilinear La Tène Celtic carving, standing in its protected enclosure in the green Galway countryside. Soft raking light picking out the swirling incised patterns; grey stone against grass and hedges, a rare link to pre-Christian Ireland.

### gal_worm_hole.webp
**The Worm Hole (Poll na bPéist)**

The Worm Hole, an almost perfectly rectangular natural pool carved by the sea into the flat limestone rock shelf of Inis Mór, its startlingly geometric shape set in the bare karst.
